Dining in Brisbane

The City of Brisbane has hundreds of amazing restaurants scattered all across the place. Whether you want Chinese, Japanese, Indian, Greek, or even Ethiopian, Brisbane has a diverse range of all cuisines to try! If you would like something easy and quick, there are hidden traditional takeaway places in the suburbs, or if you want to sit down and relax in a fine dining place, there are the known and famous restaurants.

Supernormal

image from Broadsheet

Supernormal is a fine dining pan-Asian and modern Australian restaurant located riverside in the heart of Brisbane City. It has a balance of the old school vibe while including the Modern feeling our city has, framing the story bridge and the perfect river right outside the windows. Alongside this amazing vibe, the restaurant also accommodates vegetarians, with a variety of non vegetarian dishes too. This restaurant is perfect for people that would like to sit down, and enjoy the scenic city that they are in, while eating english lobster rolls and dumplings.
