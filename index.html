Welcome the the ABS Brisbane Guidebook

Basking in over 280 days of sunshine a year, Brisbane is Australia's champion of a laid-back, subtropical lifestyle. Fondly dubbed "Brissie," Queensland's capital completely rejects the frantic rush of southern counterparts like Sydney and Melbourne, opting instead for a breezy, outdoor-centric charm. The entire city gracefully wraps around a grand, winding river, which serves as the ultimate backdrop for vibrant cultural precincts, lush urban sanctuaries, and an ever-evolving, world-class dining scene. From the bustling, beachside energy of South Bank to the creative alleyways of Fortitude Valley and Fortitude Valley's trendy pockets, Brisbane seamlessly blends metropolitan sophistication with a warm, welcoming, and distinctly relaxed energy.
